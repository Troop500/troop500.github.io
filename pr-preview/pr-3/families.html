How to Join Troop 500G

How to Join Troop 500G

Welcome to Scouting America Troop 500G! We are a female Scouting America troop operating out of Solon, Ohio, chartered by Advent Lutheran Church through the Lake Erie Council. We are happy to have you and your daughter join us for the awesome adventure of Scouting!

Visit Us at a Meeting

The best way to learn about our troop is to visit a meeting. We meet Monday evenings at Advent Lutheran Church (regular meetings 7:00-8:30 PM, summer meetings 6:30-8:00 PM at South Chagrin Reservation Shelterhouse). Check the events calendar for up-to-date details.

You may not have everything figured out when you start — don’t worry! Do your best, show up ready to learn about Scouting, and we’ll work with you on the rest.

Register with Scouting America

To join our troop, visit BeAScout.scouting.org to begin the registration process. This site will walk you through registration and help you find our unit.

What Happens Next

Once your Scout joins, here’s what to expect:

  1. Joining Conference — Every new Scout family participates in a joining conference with troop leadership before their Scout’s first overnight camping experience. This friendly meeting helps us understand your Scout’s strengths, interests, and any considerations for their success. Learn more

  2. Get Connected — We’ll add you to our GroupMe groups and make sure you have access to Scoutbook and the troop Google Calendar.

  3. Complete Required Paperwork — Annual Health and Medical Record (Parts A & B), Social Media Waiver, and Safeguarding Youth training. Details are in our Getting Started guide.

  4. Gear Up — Your Scout will need a Class A uniform, Class B troop shirt, Scout Handbook, and the 10 Essentials. We have secondhand uniform items available — just ask! Full details in our Uniform Guidelines.

Fees and Costs

Membership fees and event fees are assessed by the Treasurer and Troop Committee and communicated to families in advance. To reduce the financial burden of getting started, the initial membership fee is waived for all new members. Financial assistance is always available — contact the Scoutmaster confidentially to discuss options. No Scout is excluded due to financial constraints.

For full details, see our Financial Information page.

What Your Scout Will Experience

  • Weekly Meetings — Skill-building, advancement work, and patrol activities
  • Monthly Outings — Camping, hiking, cooking, and outdoor adventures. Most outings run Friday evening through Sunday mid-day. Camping Guidelines
  • Rank Advancement — A progressive journey from Scout rank through Eagle Scout. Advancement Overview
  • Merit Badges — Explore interests from cooking to citizenship to personal fitness. Merit Badge Info
  • Summer Camp — One week each summer at a Scout camp chosen by the troop
  • Service Projects — Community service is a cornerstone of our program
  • Leadership Development — Youth-led patrols and troop positions teach real leadership skills. Leadership Info

Attendance Matters

In order for your daughter to get the most out of Scouting, regular attendance at troop meetings, patrol meetings, and outings is essential. The troop is stronger when there is full and consistent participation.

Get Involved as a Parent

While Scouting is Scout-led, it is parent-facilitated. There are many ways to contribute — from driving to events, to helping at campouts, to serving on the committee or as an adult leader. Learn about parent involvement
