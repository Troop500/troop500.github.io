About

About Troop 500G

Troop 500G is a female Scouting America troop operating out of Solon, Ohio, serving the youth of Northeast Ohio. We are a proud part of the Lake Erie Council and are chartered by Advent Lutheran Church.

Our Mission

To provide girls with opportunities to develop leadership skills, experience outdoor adventures, serve their community, and advance toward Eagle Scout while building lifelong friendships.

Scouting America’s Foundation

Understanding Scouting America’s mission and methods helps families appreciate the comprehensive youth development program your Scout will experience.

Mission Statement

The mission of Scouting America is to prepare young people to make ethical and moral choices over their lifetimes by instilling in them the values of the Scout Oath and Law.

Vision Statement

Scouting America will prepare every eligible youth in America to become a responsible, participating citizen and leader who is guided by the Scout Oath and Law.

The Scout Oath

On my honor I will do my best To do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law; To help other people at all times; To keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.

The Scout Law

A Scout Is: Trustworthy, Loyal, Helpful, Friendly, Courteous, Kind, Obedient, Cheerful, Thrifty, Brave, Clean, Reverent

Scouting’s Aims and Methods

Scouting achieves its mission through four main aims: character development, leadership development, citizenship training, and personal fitness. These are accomplished through eight interconnected methods:

  1. Ideals - Living by the Scout Oath, Law, motto, and slogan
  2. Patrols - Small group experiences that teach responsibility and citizenship
  3. Outdoor Programs - Learning through nature and outdoor adventures
  4. Advancement - Progressive challenges that build self-confidence and skills
  5. Association with Adults - Positive role modeling and mentoring relationships
  6. Personal Growth - Individual development through service and reflection
  7. Leadership Development - Learning and practicing leadership skills
  8. Uniform - Visible commitment to Scouting ideals and community service

Our Council

Lake Erie Council, BSA
Troop 500G is proudly chartered through the Lake Erie Council, which serves Scouts across Northeast Ohio. The Lake Erie Council provides training, resources, and support to help units deliver high-quality Scouting programs that build character, citizenship, and fitness in young people.

The Lake Erie Council was formed on January 1, 2017, through the consolidation of parts of the Greater Cleveland Council, Greater Western Reserve Council, and Heart of Ohio Council, creating a unified organization that now serves the areas previously covered by these entities.
