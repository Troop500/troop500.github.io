Grubmaster Guidelines

Grubmaster Guidelines

One of the most rewarding aspects of camping is enjoying food prepared in the outdoors! As Grubmaster, you are responsible for all food-related tasks for your patrol during camping trips.

Grubmaster Responsibilities

Core Duties

  • Lead your patrol in designing suitable and healthy meal plans
  • Complete all shopping within budget constraints
  • Safely store food until the campout
  • Lead food preparation during the campout
  • Coordinate cleanup after meals
  • Ensure food safety and proper food handling

Planning Phase

Menu Development

Use the planning worksheets available:

  • Patrol Menu Planning Worksheet
  • Weekend Camping Meal Planner

Menu Considerations

  • Account for food allergies and dietary restrictions
  • Consider patrol member preferences and dislikes
  • Plan for appropriate serving sizes (typically 1.5 servings per scout)
  • Balance nutrition, taste, and cooking complexity

Recipe Selection and Scaling

  1. Research recipes online or use the troop cookbook
  2. Determine servings needed based on patrol size
  3. Calculate recipe multiples using the formula:
    • Recipe multiplier = (Number of scouts × 1.5) ÷ Recipe servings
  4. Create ingredient lists based on scaled recipes

Equipment Planning

  • Inventory needed cooking tools and equipment
  • Check troop closet, trailer, and patrol wampum box first
  • Create list of items to collect or purchase
  • Ensure all necessary equipment is available

Shopping Guidelines

Budget Management

  • Typical budget: $20 per scout for entire weekend
  • Shop at low-cost stores or choose store-brand items
  • Check troop pantry for items like crackers, marshmallows, chocolate bars
  • Buy items on sale when possible
  • Reserve approximately $3 for ice for coolers

Smart Shopping Tips

  • Only buy what you actually need
  • Consider cost-effective substitutions (e.g., store brand cheese vs. individual packages)
  • Save receipts for troop reimbursement
  • Shop 1-2 days before the campout

Food Storage

Safely store all purchased food until the campout to prevent spoilage and contamination.

During the Campout

Leadership Focus

  • Lead and delegate rather than doing all work yourself
  • Assign tasks to patrol members
  • Ensure food safety throughout preparation
  • Start meal preparation early - allow extra time

Time Management

  • Know preparation time for each meal
  • Plan for cooking to take longer than expected
  • Begin prep work well in advance of meal times
  • Coordinate with patrol’s duty roster schedule

Food Safety

  • Maintain proper food temperatures
  • Practice good hygiene during food preparation
  • Store leftovers safely or dispose of properly
  • Keep cooking area clean and organized

After the Campout

Reimbursement Process

Submit the appropriate form with all receipts to the event Scoutmaster:

  • Reimbursement Form
  • Credit Card Reimbursement Form

Forms will be processed with the treasurer for reimbursement.

Skills Development

Serving as Grubmaster helps fulfill requirements for:

  • Various rank advancement requirements
  • Merit badge requirements
  • Leadership skill development
  • Responsibility and planning skills

Tips for Success

Planning Tips

  • Start planning early - don’t wait until the last minute
  • Get input from all patrol members during menu planning
  • Have backup meal ideas in case primary plans don’t work
  • Consider weather conditions and cooking limitations

Communication

  • Keep patrol informed about meal plans and timing
  • Coordinate with patrol leader about schedule
  • Ask for help when needed - don’t try to do everything alone

Learning Opportunities

  • Each campout is a chance to improve your skills
  • Try new recipes and cooking techniques
  • Learn from experienced grubmasters in other patrols
  • Share your successes and challenges with other scouts
