Uniform Guidelines

Uniform Guidelines

Class A Uniform (Formal Uniform)

The formal Scouting America uniform is termed a “Class A” and consists of:

Required Components

Scout Uniform Shirt with dark green epaulets and the following patches:

  • Lake Erie Council patch
  • Troop numbers (500) Provided by the troop - do not purchase
  • Purple Fleur de Lis
  • Patrol Badge Provided by the troop and changes based on your patrol

Accessories:

  • Purple neckerchief with lime green edging Provided by the troop
  • SA Scout belt (some pants/shorts have built-in belts)
  • Scout Uniform Pants or zip-off pants (uniform shorts for summer)

Scout Handbook: Required for all scouts Provided for girls crossing over from Cubs

Where to Purchase

Items can be purchased at the Scout Shop at Lake Erie Council Headquarters: Address: 2241 Woodland Ave, Cleveland, Ohio
Note: All pants and shorts come un-hemmed

Used Uniforms Available

We maintain a collection of Class A uniforms that scouts have outgrown. Contact leadership if you’d like to try these before purchasing new ones.

Class A Uniform Required Events

  • Troop Meetings
  • Court of Honor
  • Summer Camp
  • Scoutmaster Conferences
  • Boards of Review
  • Other occasions as announced

Class B Uniform (Activity Uniform)

For other troop activities, a Troop 500G t-shirt (Class B) can be worn with:

  • Scout pants
  • Jeans
  • Shorts

Purchase: Class B shirts are purchased through the troop.

Patch Placement

Proper placement of patches is detailed in the Scout Handbook. Ensure patches are sewn on securely and in the correct positions.

Care Instructions

  • Follow washing instructions on garment labels
  • Iron patches carefully to avoid damage
  • Store uniforms properly to maintain appearance
  • Replace worn or damaged items promptly

Financial Assistance

If cost is a concern, please speak confidentially with the Scoutmaster about assistance options.
