Youth Leadership Positions

Youth Leadership Positions

Troop 500G operates using the patrol method with youth-led leadership. Elections are held every 6 months for key positions.

Eligibility Requirements

To run for troop and patrol leadership positions, scouts must:

  • Hold a rank of First Class or higher
  • Have been active in a scout troop for at least six months
  • Demonstrate leadership potential and commitment

Troop Leadership Positions

Senior Patrol Leader (SPL)

Election Schedule: Every 6 months
Responsibilities:

  • Lead troop meetings and activities
  • Coordinate with adult leadership
  • Chair Patrol Leader Council meetings
  • Represent the troop at council events

Assistant Senior Patrol Leader (ASPL)

Responsibilities:

  • Assist the Senior Patrol Leader
  • Step in when SPL is unavailable
  • Lead specific troop programs as assigned
  • Help coordinate patrol activities

Troop Scribe

Responsibilities:

  • Keep attendance records
  • Record meeting minutes
  • Maintain troop records
  • Assist with communications

Troop Quartermaster

Responsibilities:

  • Manage troop equipment and gear
  • Coordinate gear checkout and return
  • Maintain inventory lists
  • Oversee gear maintenance procedures

Patrol Leadership Positions

Patrol Leader

Election: Within each patrol Responsibilities:

  • Lead patrol meetings and activities
  • Represent patrol at Patrol Leader Council
  • Coordinate patrol camping and cooking
  • Mentor newer scouts in the patrol

Assistant Patrol Leader

Responsibilities:

  • Assist the Patrol Leader
  • Lead when Patrol Leader is absent
  • Help coordinate patrol activities
  • Support patrol gear management

Position of Responsibility Requirements

Many leadership positions fulfill the “position of responsibility” requirement for rank advancement. Scouts in these positions are expected to:

  • Attend Patrol Leader Council meetings monthly
  • Fulfill their position duties consistently
  • Show leadership and mentorship to other scouts
  • Participate actively in planning troop activities

Patrol Leader Council (PLC)

Who Attends: All patrol leaders, troop leadership, and invited adult leaders
Frequency: At least monthly
Purpose: Plan troop meetings, activities, and outings

PLC Responsibilities

  • Plan and organize troop activities
  • Coordinate inter-patrol activities
  • Address troop issues and concerns
  • Plan Courts of Honor and special events

Leadership Training

All youth leaders are expected to complete appropriate leadership training for their positions. Training opportunities include:

  • Introduction to Leadership Skills for Troops (ILST)
  • National Youth Leadership Training (NYLT)
  • Council and troop-specific leadership development

Adult Support

While troops are youth-led, adult leaders provide guidance and support to help youth leaders succeed. The Scoutmaster and Assistant Scoutmasters work closely with youth leaders to mentor and develop their skills.
