# About Troop 500G

Troop 500G is a female Scouting America troop operating out of Solon, Ohio, serving the youth of Northeast Ohio. We are a proud part of the Lake Erie Council and are chartered by Advent Lutheran Church.

## Our Mission
To provide girls with opportunities to develop leadership skills, experience outdoor adventures, serve their community, and advance toward Eagle Scout while building lifelong friendships.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Troop 500G welcomes all youth who want to participate in Scouting, regardless of ability, background, or circumstance. We adapt activities and provide reasonable accommodations so every Scout can participate meaningfully. If your Scout has specific needs, please reach out to the Scoutmaster or Committee Chair so we can work together on a plan.

For more information, see the [Scouting America Accessibility and Inclusion Information Booklet]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/files/handbook/attachments/Accessibility%20and%20Inclusion%20Committee-%20Information%20Booklet.pdf).
