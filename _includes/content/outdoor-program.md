# Outdoor Program

Our outdoor program is the heart of Troop 500G's activities, providing Scouts with opportunities to learn outdoor skills, experience adventure, and develop self-reliance.

## Monthly Camping

We camp monthly, year-round, in all weather conditions. Our camping program includes:

- **Car camping** at established campgrounds
- **Backpacking** adventures
- **Winter camping** for cold-weather skills
- **High adventure** expeditions

## Essential Gear

Every Scout should have their own basic camping equipment. See our [Ten Essentials guide](/ten-essentials) and [Tent Camping List](/tent-camping-list) for detailed packing information.

## Grubmaster Program

Our unique grubmaster system teaches Scouts meal planning, budgeting, and cooking skills. Each Scout takes turns being responsible for planning and preparing meals for the entire troop during camping trips.

Resources:
- [Grubmaster 101](/grubmaster-101) - Basic meal planning
- [Grubmaster 201](/grubmaster-201) - Advanced cooking techniques

## High Adventure

Older Scouts have opportunities to participate in high adventure programs including:
- Philmont Scout Ranch
- Northern Tier Canoe Base
- Florida Sea Base
- Summit Bechtel Reserve

## Leave No Trace

All outdoor activities follow Leave No Trace principles to minimize our environmental impact and preserve natural areas for future generations.
