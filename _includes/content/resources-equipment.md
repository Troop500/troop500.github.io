# Resources & Equipment

## Troop Equipment

The troop maintains a variety of equipment available for Scout use:

### Camping Equipment
- Tents and tarps
- Cooking stoves and fuel
- First aid supplies
- Patrol cooking equipment

### Activity Equipment
- Game and activity supplies
- Merit badge materials
- Ceremonial items

View our complete equipment inventory in the [Files section](/files).

## Equipment Maintenance

Regular maintenance of troop equipment is essential for safety and longevity. Scouts participate in equipment maintenance as part of their service hours and skill development.

See our [Troop Gear Maintenance](/troop-gear-maintenance) guide for detailed maintenance procedures.

## Personal Equipment

Scouts are responsible for acquiring and maintaining their own personal camping equipment. Essential items include:

- Sleeping bag and pad
- Backpack
- Rain gear
- Personal camping items

See our [Ten Essentials](/ten-essentials) and [Tent Camping List](/tent-camping-list) for complete packing guidance.

## Duty Roster

All troop members participate in setup, cleanup, and maintenance activities through our [duty roster system](/duty-roster). This teaches responsibility and ensures that everyone contributes to troop operations.
