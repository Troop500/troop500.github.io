**Lake Erie Council, BSA**  
Troop 500G is proudly part of the Lake Erie Council, which serves Scouts across Northeast Ohio. The Lake Erie Council provides training, resources, and support to help units deliver high-quality Scouting programs that build character, citizenship, and fitness in young people.

The Lake Erie Council was formed on January 1, 2017, through the consolidation of parts of the Greater Cleveland Council, Greater Western Reserve Council, and Heart of Ohio Council, creating a unified organization that now serves the areas previously covered by these entities.
