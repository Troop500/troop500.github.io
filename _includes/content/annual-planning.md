# Annual Planning

Troop 500G follows a structured annual planning cycle that keeps our program organized, Scout-led, and focused on delivering a quality Scouting experience throughout the year.

## Elections and Leadership Transitions

### Senior Patrol Leader Elections
The Senior Patrol Leader (SPL) is elected by the troop every six months. Candidates must meet the following eligibility requirements:

- Hold a rank of First Class or higher
- Have been active in a Scout troop for at least six months

### Patrol and Troop Position Elections
Patrol Leaders are elected by their patrol members and other troop positions are appointed by the SPL with the advice and counsel of the Scoutmaster. Experienced Scouts holding a rank of First Class or higher, and having been active in a Scout troop for at least six months, will have an opportunity to run for patrol and troop positions.

Elections provide Scouts with real-world experience in democratic processes and give every eligible Scout the opportunity to serve in a leadership role.

## Patrol Leaders' Council (PLC)

Those elected to leadership positions are expected to participate in Patrol Leaders' Conferences (PLC), which are held at least monthly. The PLC is the troop's planning and coordination body, led by the Senior Patrol Leader.

### PLC Responsibilities
- Plan troop meetings and activities
- Coordinate the monthly outing calendar
- Discuss troop business and address concerns
- Plan service projects and special events
- Review and adjust the annual program plan

## Annual Program Planning Conference

Each year, the troop holds an annual program planning conference where youth leaders — guided by adult mentors — build the troop's calendar for the coming year. This conference sets the direction for:

- Monthly camping trips and outings
- Merit badge opportunities and advancement activities
- Service projects and community involvement
- Special events, courts of honor, and celebrations
- Summer camp selection
- High adventure planning for eligible Scouts

## Program Cycle Overview

Our troop program generally follows the Scouting year cycle:

- **Fall**: New year kickoff, recruitment, elections, and outdoor skill-building campouts
- **Winter**: Indoor skill sessions, winter camping, and merit badge focus
- **Spring**: Advancement push, service projects, and preparation for summer activities
- **Summer**: Summer camp, high adventure trips, and outdoor exploration

*The specific calendar is planned annually by the Patrol Leaders' Council during the annual program planning conference. Check the [events calendar](/events) and Scoutbook for the current schedule.*
