# Financial Information

Understanding the financial aspects of Scouting helps families plan and participate fully in the troop's activities and programs.

## Membership Fees

There are two separate annual fees every Scout and registered adult must pay. They are billed by different organizations, paid through different methods, and serve different purposes.

### Scouting America Registration Fees (Annual)
This fee is paid **directly to Scouting America** — not to the troop.

- **Where to pay**: [my.scouting.org](https://my.scouting.org) — log in to your Scouting America account and complete annual membership renewal
- **What it covers**: Your official Scouting America membership, council affiliation, and liability insurance for all Scouting activities
- **Important**: Registration lapses if not renewed annually. Scouts and adults who are not currently registered cannot participate in official Scouting activities.
- **Questions**: Contact Scouting America or the Lake Erie Council for assistance with your account

### Troop Dues (Annual)
This fee is paid **to Troop 500** and is separate from your Scouting America registration.

- **Where to pay**: By check made payable to **Troop 500**, delivered to the Treasurer at a meeting or event
- **Amount**: Assessed by the Treasurer and Troop Committee and communicated to families in advance
- **Payment schedule**: Announced by the committee with payment reminders
- **What it covers**: Court of Honor supplies and recognition items, equipment and gear maintenance, program materials and supplies, and general troop operations
- **Questions**: Contact the Treasurer or Committee Chair for current due amounts and deadlines

## Payment Methods

Checks are the preferred method of payment for troop fees and expenses.

- **Make checks payable to**: Troop 500
- Deliver checks to the Treasurer or an authorized troop leader at a meeting or event
- Contact the Treasurer to arrange other payment arrangements if needed

## Event Sign-Up and Cancellation Policy

Scoutmasters will announce a sign-up due date for each outing. Timely RSVPs are critical — we reserve campsites, register scouts, and plan food based on confirmed attendance.

### Sign-Up Deadlines
- RSVP through Scoutbook by the due date announced for each event
- A Google Form may also be required; the link will be provided in Scoutbook
- **Scouts who have not signed up by the deadline will not be able to attend**

### Cancellation After Sign-Up
- Scouts who have signed up are financially committed to the event
- If unable to attend after signing up, the Scout is responsible for **paying the fee or finding a replacement**
- This policy exists because costs are committed in advance on behalf of each registered Scout

## Scout Dollars

Scout Dollars are credits held in your Scout's individual troop account. They work like a balance that can be applied toward future fees and expenses.

### How Scout Dollars Are Earned
Credits are added to your Scout's account for any of the following reasons:

- **Overpayment**: If a fee collected exceeds the actual cost of an event, the difference is credited to your Scout's account
- **Campership Awards**: Financial awards received from the council or other sources are credited as Scout Dollars
- **Fundraising**: Earnings from troop fundraising activities where individual credit is applied (e.g., Yard Charge proceeds)
- **Other Credits**: Any other approved credit at the discretion of the Treasurer or Committee

### Using Scout Dollars
- Scout Dollar balances can be applied toward any troop fee or expense
- The Treasurer maintains records of each Scout's balance
- Contact the Treasurer to inquire about your Scout's current balance

## Budget Planning Tips

### For Families
- Plan for regular membership fees as communicated by the Treasurer
- Budget for monthly outing costs
