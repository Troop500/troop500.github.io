# Scout Camps We Frequent

Our troop has experience with many excellent Scout camps throughout Ohio and the surrounding region. These camps offer diverse programs, beautiful settings, and memorable experiences for our Scouts.

**Recent Summer Camps:**
- **2025**: Beaumont Scout Reservation, Seven Ranges Scout Reservation
- **2026**: Pioneer Scout Reservation *(scheduled)*

**Special Events:**
- **2026**: National Jamboree at Summit Bechtel Reserve *(contingent participating)*

## Lake Erie Council Camps

### [Beaumont Scout Reservation](https://www.beaumontscoutreservation.org/)
Lake Erie Council's Scout reservation offering unique programs and outdoor experiences, providing Scouts with opportunities to develop new skills and create lasting memories.

### [Firelands Scout Reservation](https://lecscouting.org/firelands)
Lake Erie Council Scout reservation featuring diverse programming options, outdoor adventure activities, and excellent facilities for summer camp and weekend events.

## Great Trail Council Camps

### [Manatoc Scout Reservation](https://www.manatoc.org)
Great Trail Council's premier Scout reservation featuring aquatic programs, traditional Scout camping, and a wide variety of merit badge opportunities in a scenic lakeside location.

### [Camp Stambaugh](https://www.manatoc.org/camping/camp-stambaugh)
A premier Scout camp located in Mineral Ridge, Ohio, within the Manatoc Scout Reservation under Great Trail Council. Camp Stambaugh offers comprehensive summer camp programs, weekend camping, and outdoor adventure activities in a beautiful wooded setting.

### [Camp Butler](https://www.manatoc.org/camping/camp-butler)
Located within the Manatoc Scout Reservation, Camp Butler offers summer programs and outdoor adventures with a focus on traditional Scouting skills and leadership development.

## Buckeye Council Camps

### [Seven Ranges Scout Reservation](https://www.buckeyecouncil.org/camping-old/weekend-camping/seven-ranges-scout-reservation/)
Buckeye Council's Scout reservation offering comprehensive summer camp programs and outdoor adventure activities. Our troop participated in summer camp here in 2025, experiencing their excellent facilities and programming.

## Erie Shores Council Camps

### [Pioneer Scout Reservation](https://www.psrweb.org/)
Erie Shores Council's Pioneer Scout Reservation includes Camp Fontier and offers excellent summer camp programs with a focus on outdoor skills, leadership development, and traditional Scouting experiences.

## Independent and Regional Camps

### [Camp Tuscazoar](https://www.tuscazoar.org/)
Founded more than a century ago, Camp Tuscazoar is now operated by the Camp Tuscazoar Foundation as an Ohio non-profit corporation. This historic camp continues to serve Scouts and youth organizations while also welcoming weekend campers, hikers, mountain bikers, and horse riders throughout the year.

### [Laurel Highlands High Adventure](https://www.golaurelhighlands.com/)
A high adventure destination we're interested in visiting in the future. Laurel Highlands offers exciting outdoor adventure programs and activities that provide Scouts with opportunities to challenge themselves and develop confidence in wilderness settings.

## Local Outdoor Adventure Partners

### [Camp Hi Adventure](https://www.camphiadventure.com/)
A privately-owned outdoor adventure facility on the Cuyahoga River, operated by the Hazlett family since 1928. Our troop has historically participated in family canoe and kayak events following the annual Twins Day Festival. With over 250 canoes, kayaks, and paddle boards, Camp Hi provides "Adventure in Your Own Backyard" experiences that promote the restorative benefits of river recreation.

## National High Adventure Bases

For comprehensive information about all National High Adventure Bases, visit [Scouting America's High Adventure page](https://www.scouting.org/national-high-adventure-bases/).

### [Philmont Scout Ranch](https://www.philmontscoutranch.org/)
Our Scouts and Scouters have participated in treks at Philmont Scout Ranch, Scouting America's premier high adventure base in New Mexico. This 140,000-acre wilderness ranch offers life-changing backpacking experiences that challenge Scouts physically, mentally, and spiritually while building leadership skills and lifelong memories.

### [Sea Base](https://seabaseha.org/)
Scouting America's aquatic high adventure base in the Florida Keys offers unique maritime adventures including sailing, snorkeling, scuba diving, and marine conservation programs in some of the most beautiful waters in the world.

### [Northern Tier](https://www.ntier.org/)
Located in Minnesota's Boundary Waters Canoe Area Wilderness, Northern Tier provides canoe and backpacking adventures in pristine wilderness settings, offering Scouts the opportunity to experience untouched nature and develop self-reliance skills.

## Camp Selection Process

Our troop chooses different camps each summer based on several factors:
- **Scout Preferences**: We let our Scouts' interests drive our camp selection
- **Program Offerings**: Different camps excel in different areas (aquatics, high adventure, traditional skills, etc.)
- **New Experiences**: We like to expose our Scouts to different camp environments and programs
- **Quality and Safety**: All selected camps maintain high standards for safety and program quality

## Planning Your Camp Experience

**Before Camp:**
- Visit camp websites to learn about their programs and facilities
- Check what merit badges and activities will be available
- Prepare by reviewing Scout skills and requirements

**During Camp:**
- Participate fully in all activities and programs
- Work on advancement opportunities
- Build friendships with Scouts from other troops
- Embrace new challenges and experiences

**After Camp:**
- Share your experiences with the troop
- Complete any follow-up requirements for merit badges
- Apply skills learned at camp to future troop activities
