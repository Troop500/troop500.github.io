# Merit Badge Program

Merit badges are an essential part of the scouting experience, allowing scouts to explore interests, develop skills, and earn recognition for their achievements.

## Merit Badge Requirements

### Eagle Scout Requirements
- **Total merit badges needed**: 21 minimum for Eagle Scout rank
- **Required merit badges**: 14 specific badges are required
- **Elective merit badges**: 7 additional badges of the scout's choice

## Merit Badge Process

### Getting Started
1. **Blue Card**: Essential documentation for permission to begin and completion record
   - Three-part card: Troop copy, Counselor copy, Scout copy
   - Scout must organize and preserve blue cards as record of accomplishments
   - Print blue cards from Scoutbook periodically as backup

### Working with Merit Badge Counselors
- Scouts work with approved merit badge counselors
- May complete badges during merit badge universities
- Summer camp offers many merit badge opportunities
- Individual work with counselors also available

### Documentation Methods
- **Electronic tracking**: Primarily use Scoutbook.com for records
- **Physical blue cards**: ONLY acceptable document if computer records are damaged
- **Backup recommended**: Print blue cards periodically for safety

## Merit Badge Resources

### Merit Badge Books
- Books specific to each merit badge contain detailed information
- Content available online - physical books not required
- Books may be purchased at Scout Shop (optional)
- Available for borrowing from:
  - Troop Library
  - Local libraries

### Completion Process
- Merit badge counselor presents scout copy of blue card upon completion
- Troop retains one part for records
- Merit badge counselor keeps one part

## Important Safeguards

### Record Protection
Because blue cards are the ONLY acceptable documentation if computer records are lost:
- Keep physical copies of blue cards in safe place
- Regularly print blue cards from Scoutbook
- Maintain backup documentation of all merit badge work

### Quality Control
- All merit badge requirements must be fully completed
- Counselors verify completion before signing blue cards
- Scouts must demonstrate proficiency in required skills

## Merit Badge Opportunities

### Regular Opportunities
- **Merit Badge Universities**: Council-sponsored events with multiple merit badges offered
- **Summer Camp**: Week-long resident camp with extensive merit badge program  
- **Individual Counselors**: Work one-on-one with approved counselors
- **Troop Programs**: Some merit badges offered during troop activities

### Planning Your Merit Badge Journey
- Review required badges for Eagle Scout early
- Choose elective badges based on personal interests
- Plan ahead for badges requiring extended time or special equipment
- Take advantage of camp and university opportunities

## Merit Badge Categories

Merit badges cover diverse areas including:
- **Outdoor Skills**: Camping, hiking, wilderness survival
- **Life Skills**: Cooking, personal management, communication
- **Arts & Crafts**: Art, music, theater, photography
- **Science & Technology**: Engineering, robotics, environmental science
- **Sports & Fitness**: Athletics, sports, physical fitness
- **Community Service**: Citizenship, volunteering, service projects

## Getting Help

### Resources Available
- Merit badge counselor list maintained by troop
- Advancement coordinator available for questions
- Scoutbook training and support available
- Fellow scouts who have earned badges can provide guidance

### Adult Support
Adult leaders can help with:
- Finding appropriate merit badge counselors
- Understanding merit badge requirements
- Tracking progress toward advancement goals
- Coordinating group merit badge opportunities
