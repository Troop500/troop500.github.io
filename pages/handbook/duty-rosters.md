# Duty Rosters & Positions

A duty roster is an essential organizational tool that ensures all necessary jobs are completed during campouts and activities. Each patrol creates and manages their own duty roster.

## What is a Duty Roster?

### Definition
A duty roster is a list of jobs that need to be completed during a campout, with specific scouts assigned to each responsibility.

### Purpose
- Ensures all necessary tasks are completed
- Distributes work fairly among patrol members
- Provides leadership opportunities
- Helps scouts learn new skills
- Maintains organized and efficient camp operations

## Creating a Duty Roster

### Patrol Leader's Responsibility
The patrol leader creates the duty roster by:
- Determining who is attending the campout
- Assessing individual scouts' skills and experience
- Considering rank advancement needs
- Rotating assignments fairly among patrol members
- Matching scouts to appropriate responsibilities

### Considerations for Assignment
- **Skill development**: Match scouts to positions they need for advancement
- **Experience level**: Pair experienced scouts with newer members
- **Merit badge requirements**: Assign positions that fulfill specific requirements
- **Fairness**: Rotate challenging and easy assignments
- **Leadership opportunities**: Give scouts chances to lead

## Standard Duty Roster Positions

### Water Master
**Responsibilities:**
- Ensure adequate water supply for patrol
- Monitor water quality and safety
- Coordinate water collection and storage
- Maintain water containers and equipment

### Fire Master
**Responsibilities:**
- Build and maintain campfires safely
- Ensure proper fire safety protocols
- Manage fire supplies (wood, kindling, matches)
- Extinguish fires completely when finished
- Supervise fire-related activities

**Requirements:**
- Must have completed Firem'n Chit training
- Demonstrates fire safety knowledge
- Capable of teaching fire skills to others

### Head Cook (Usually Grubmaster)
**Responsibilities:**
- Plan and coordinate all meals
- Lead food preparation activities
- Ensure food safety and proper cooking
- Coordinate with assistant cook
- Manage cooking timeline and logistics

**Benefits:**
- Fulfills rank advancement requirements
- Counts toward merit badge work
- Develops leadership and planning skills

### Assistant Cook
**Responsibilities:**
- Support head cook in meal preparation
- Assist with food preparation and cooking
- Help coordinate cooking activities
- Learn cooking skills and techniques
- Support meal service and timing

### Campsite Cleanup
**Responsibilities:**
- Maintain clean and organized campsite
- Coordinate general campsite tidiness
- Organize gear and equipment storage
- Ensure Leave No Trace principles
- Lead campsite setup and breakdown

### Kitchen Cleanup
**Responsibilities:**
- Clean cooking equipment and utensils
- Manage dishwashing and sanitizing
- Organize cooking supplies and storage
- Maintain clean food preparation areas
- Dispose of food waste properly

## Additional Positions

### Patrol-Specific Additions
Patrols may add additional positions based on:
- Specific campout activities
- Special equipment needs
- Merit badge opportunities
- Leadership development goals
- Group size and dynamics

### Example Additional Positions
- **Gear Master**: Responsible for patrol equipment
- **Navigation Leader**: Handles maps and route planning
- **Safety Officer**: Monitors safety procedures
- **Communication Leader**: Manages patrol communications
- **Activity Coordinator**: Organizes patrol activities

## Duty Roster Management

### Before the Campout
- Create roster during patrol meetings
- Ensure all scouts understand their responsibilities
- Review specific requirements for each position
- Provide training or guidance as needed
- Confirm attendance and adjust assignments

### During the Campout
- Post duty roster where all can see it
- Monitor completion of assigned tasks
- Provide support and guidance as needed
- Make adjustments if attendance changes
- Recognize good performance

### After the Campout
- Review how well assignments worked
- Discuss improvements for next time
- Plan rotation for future rosters
- Document successes and challenges

## Leadership Development Through Duty Rosters

### Skill Building
Duty rosters provide opportunities to develop:
- **Leadership skills**: Leading others in specific tasks
- **Responsibility**: Being accountable for important jobs
- **Time management**: Completing tasks on schedule
- **Teamwork**: Working with others effectively
- **Problem-solving**: Handling challenges that arise

### Rank Advancement
Many duty roster positions help fulfill:
- Rank advancement requirements
- Merit badge prerequisites
- Leadership experience requirements
- Skill demonstration opportunities

### Progressive Development
- Start with simpler assignments for new scouts
- Gradually increase responsibility and complexity
- Provide mentorship from experienced scouts
- Encourage teaching others what they've learned
